## Facebook / community post

🎧💜 **Introducing LokiTunes v1.0.0!** 🩵

My free, open-source **offline music player for Android** is officially available on GitHub!

Enjoy the songs already saved on your phone with a clean Midnight Aurora interface, folder browsing, Favorites, custom playlists, Smart Lyrics, a Sound Studio equalizer (device permitting), and in-app updates. 🎶

✅ Offline music playback  
✅ No ads or account needed  
✅ Favorites, playlists and folder browsing  
✅ Free APK + source code

📥 Download: https://github.com/LokiJ07/LokiTunes/releases/latest

Requires Android 8.0+. Online lyrics search and update checks require internet; playing local music does not. Device compatibility is still being improved—feedback and bug reports are welcome!

**LokiTunes — Your Music. Your Rules.** 🎧
